Yingchen Yu

Research Scientist  ·  Ph.D., Nanyang Technological University

I was a Research Scientist @ TikTok/ByteDance, Singapore. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), supervised by Prof. Shijian Lu, under the Alibaba–NTU Industrial Postgraduate Programme (IPP).

Before that, I obtained my B.E. degree in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at NTU, and my M.S. degree in Computer Science at National University of Singapore (NUS).

Research Interests

  • Visual Generation
  • Multimodal LLMs
  • GUI Agents
